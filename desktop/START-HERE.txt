PROOFPAGE — Your sources. Your evidence.

Windows: Extract the entire ZIP, then double-click Proofpage.exe.
macOS: Extract the ZIP, drag Proofpage.app to Applications, then open it.
Linux: Extract the archive, then open the Proofpage executable in a graphical desktop.

Python is included. No terminal, account, or subscription is needed.
Your workspace opens in its own native Tauri window. Keep Proofpage open for
scheduled collection. Closing the window stops the local engine after active work finishes.
Settings → App updates checks for releases and offers the download for your computer.
Updates currently use download-and-replace, not automatic installation.

Your data is saved in the Proofpage folder inside your user folder, separately from
this application. Keep all extracted application files together on Windows/Linux.
To upgrade, close the app and replace its application files; keep your data folder.

Official Mac releases are Developer ID signed and Apple-notarized. Windows packages
remain unsigned and may show a publisher warning. Do not disable system protections.
Linux requires a graphical desktop and WebKitGTK 4.1 runtime (Ubuntu 22.04+).
Windows uses Microsoft Edge WebView2; install its official runtime if unavailable.

Documentation: https://github.com/Mehxeo/Proofpage-OpenSource
