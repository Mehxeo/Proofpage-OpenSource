PROOFPAGE — Your sources. Your evidence.

Windows: Extract the entire ZIP, then double-click Proofpage.exe.
macOS: Extract the ZIP, drag Proofpage.app to Applications, then open it.
Linux: Extract the archive, then open the Proofpage executable in a graphical desktop.

Python is included. No terminal, account, or subscription is needed.
Your workspace opens in your default browser. Keep the small Proofpage window open.
Use Open workspace to return to it, or Show data folder to find your saved files.
Close the Proofpage window to stop the server. Active captures may need time to finish.

Your data is saved in the Proofpage folder inside your user folder, separately from
this application. Keep all extracted application files together on Windows/Linux.
To upgrade, close the app and replace its application files; keep your data folder.

This is an unsigned preview. Your operating system may display an unknown-publisher
warning or block launch. Developer signing and Apple notarization are not yet configured.
Do not disable your system's security protections.

Documentation: https://github.com/Mehxeo/Proofpage-OpenSource
