A few things to know.
Is Proofpage free?
Yes. The local app is free and MIT licensed. You can inspect, modify, and run the source yourself. Hosted service plans are separate future work; no paid account is needed here.
Does my evidence leave my computer?
Your workspace is stored locally. Collection contacts the public sites you choose. Model review sends selected content only when you request it; optional timestamp anchoring sends a digest. Exported files are yours to share. This homepage has no analytics or signup form.
Does “verified” mean the answer is true?
No. Claim Check compares wording. Receipts attest what your workspace observed, not the truth of a statement or proof that the server sent it. Publisher signals are evidence to review, not a legal determination.
Can it capture every website?
No. Static pages and supported supplied archives work best. Robots policies and limits are respected. For signed-in pages, use the optional browser companion after signing in and completing any challenge yourself. Missing resources and access failures are reported. Browser capture guide ↗
How do I update without losing my library?
Back up your database first, then replace the application files. Your desktop workspace lives separately in your user folder under Proofpage/data. Keep backups private: they include your receipt-signing key.
