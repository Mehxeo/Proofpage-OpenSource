# Rights Radar

Every new web capture records observable publisher signals alongside the evidence: AI crawler robots rules, Content-Usage, Content-Signal, TDMRep, noai/noimageai, recorded license links and recognized license metadata. Open a record's **Rights** tab to inspect the evidence behind its training, AI-use and search verdicts. Draft standards and conflicting signals are not a legal determination.

**Unknown is not permission.** A reservation blocks export unless you record a rights basis and explanation; unknown signals require acknowledgment. Own content, licensing and public-domain decisions remain the user's responsibility. Imported archives are labeled user supplied, not independently cleared for training.

A budgeted `/.well-known/tdmrep.json` check runs at most once per origin per day. License URLs are recorded without automatically fetching arbitrary linked documents. Rights changes appear in Timeline and flag affected frozen datasets. Revocation blocks future downloads, but cannot recall copies already exported.

Training bundles include rights evidence and draft documentation aids: a training-content summary, dataset card and Croissant metadata. Review them before publication. They do not certify compliance with any law, and automated observation cannot settle copyright, contractual or privacy rights.

Use `proofpage rights SOURCE_ID` for JSON, or MCP `proofpage_get_rights(item_id)`. Cited-context results include observed rights so agents can surface restrictions.
